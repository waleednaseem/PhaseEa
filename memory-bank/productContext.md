# Phase — Product Context

## Why
Traders need a clear visual of whether price is in a bullish RSI bounce band (35–80) or bearish band (20–65), including historical context.

## How it should feel
- Professional dark green/red tints — obvious but not neon
- RSI pane levels always visible
- History strips show when regime flipped
- Body boxes highlight open–close structure after pullback candles

## UX
Attach EA to chart → Phase_RSI appears → background + strips + boxes update on new bars. Live bounce trades when enabled (lot/SL/TP inputs).
