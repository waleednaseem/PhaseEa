# Phase — Project Brief

## What
RSI-based **visual regime Expert Advisor** for MetaTrader 5. Detects bullish/bearish phases from RSI(10) band occupancy and paints chart background, history strips, RSI pane zones, and body boxes.

## Core goals
1. CB sticky regime: bull hold ≥40 after >65 break / bear fail ≤65 after <40 break
2. RSI(14); BUY green / SELL red / END grey on flips
3. History strips + body boxes + Phase_RSI (40–50 / 55–65 zones)

## Non-goals
Trading, risk management, Docker, modifying sibling EAs.
